## 🛒 Olist Marketplace Analytics

### Sales, Revenue, and Delivery Performance

**SQL Server · Power BI · Excel · Data Modeling · Data Validation**

#### Business Problem

Olist needed a consolidated view of marketplace performance across orders, products, customers, sellers, payments, and deliveries. I transformed fragmented transactional data into an analytical model and dashboards for monitoring commercial and operational KPIs.

#### What I Did

- Cleaned, connected, and validated data from multiple relational tables.
- Built a star schema with a sales fact table and its corresponding dimensions.
- Reconciled revenue totals and checked for duplicates and referential integrity.
- Created Power BI dashboards to analyze revenue, orders, categories, geography, and logistics.
- Translated the results into recommendations for market expansion and delivery improvement.

#### Key Findings

| KPI / Finding | Result |
|:---|---:|
| Product revenue | **R$13.22 million** |
| Delivered orders | **96,478** |
| Average order value | **R$137.04** |
| Leading category | **Health and Beauty — R$1.23 million** |
| São Paulo’s share of revenue | **38.3%** |
| Average delivery time | **12 days** |
| Late deliveries | **8.11%** |

### Sales, Customer, Product, and Territory Analysis

**SQL · Power BI · Python · Databricks · PySpark · AWS S3 · Airflow**

#### Business Problem

AdventureWorks needed a unified reporting solution to understand sales performance and identify the products, territories, customers, and salespeople that contributed the most to revenue.

#### What I Did

## 📈 LearnLoop Marketing Analytics

### Marketing Funnel, Customer Acquisition, and Revenue Analysis

**SQL · Tableau · Snowflake · dbt · Python · Airflow · AWS S3**

#### Business Problem

LearnLoop needed to understand whether advertising investment was generating customers and revenue, rather than only clicks and sessions. I created an analytical workflow that connects marketing activity with CRM, subscription, customer, and revenue data.

#### What I Did

- Integrated data from advertising, the website, CRM, subscriptions, and revenue sources.
- Organized SQL transformations into **staging, intermediate, and marts** layers using dbt.
- Defined financial and funnel KPIs, including conversion rates, CAC, ROAS, MRR, and ARR.
- Built Tableau dashboards to analyze campaigns, funnel performance, customer value, and courses.
- Identified inefficient marketing investment and opportunities to improve conversion.

#### Key Findings

| KPI | Result |
|:---|---:|
| Marketing investment | **$1.11 million** |
| Attributed revenue | **$373.5K** |
| Return on ad spend | **0.34x** |
| Customer acquisition cost | **$116.08** |
| Website sessions | **740,283** |
| Leads | **93,276** |
| Customers | **9,592** |
| Lead-to-customer conversion | **10.3%** |
